Human-centered AI researcher

Zilin Ma, Ph.D.

I build and study AI systems for high-stakes work.

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Harvard Business School AI Institute (formerly the D³ Institute), working with Jacqueline N. Lane, Shai Bernstein, and Karim R. Lakhani. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Harvard SEAS in 2025, advised by Prof. Krzysztof Gajos.

I study how AI systems change judgment and agency, especially in settings where mistakes matter. My work includes LLM tools deployed with humanitarian negotiators from the UN, ICRC, and MSF; evaluations of mental-health chatbots with LGBTQ+ communities; and interfaces designed to reduce implicit bias. My work on LLM chatbots for mental well-being was an AMIA Best Student Paper Finalist.
