How LGBTQ+ people use LLM chatbots for mental health support

2023 - 2024

Study context

People use chatbots such as Replika and Character.ai for companionship, reflection, and advice. We studied these uses with particular attention to LGBTQ+ people, who may also use the systems to explore identity or discuss experiences of discrimination.

Research questions

  • What kinds of support do users seek from LLM chatbots?
  • How do LGBTQ+ users' goals and experiences differ from those of other users?
  • Where do chatbot responses fail to address LGBTQ+-specific needs?
120

Reddit posts analyzed, containing 2,917 comments

31

In-depth interviews conducted (18 LGBTQ+, 13 non-LGBTQ+)

My role

I formulated the research questions and led data collection, interviews, coding, and analysis. Co-authors contributed to the interview protocol, codebook validation, and papers; senior advisors provided methodological guidance.

Methods and results

Methods

Online Ethnography

Analyzed 120 posts and 2,917 comments from the r/Replika subreddit to identify user needs, patterns, and emotional outcomes.

In-Depth Interviews

Conducted 31 interviews (18 LGBTQ+, 13 non-LGBTQ+) to explore diverse user goals and experiences with AI chatbots.

Analysis

We used open coding and thematic analysis. The study received IRB approval, and reported data were anonymized.

Findings

  • Participants described companionship, reduced loneliness, and opportunities to rehearse social interactions
  • LGBTQ+ users used chatbots to explore identity, rehearse coming out, and process discrimination
  • Risks included emotional over-reliance, inappropriate advice, and stigma around AI companionship
